Basic Enemies

Common foes found throughout Tenebrous Isle

Note: Enemy information is based on official wiki data and media reviews. More enemy types will be added as they are confirmed and documented.
💧

Gooper

Basic

Goopers are gelatinous creatures found across Tenebrous Isle. These slimes are native to the island but have multiplied rapidly as the corruption spread. On their own, a single Gooper poses little threat -- their movements are sluggish and they attack with simple leaps. However, when several of them appear together, they can quickly box you in and overwhelm you with numbers.

Type
Slime
Threat
Low (High in groups)
Behavior
Slow, jumping

Combat Tips

  • They creep forward at a leisurely pace and occasionally hop toward you
  • Easy to deal with one-on-one, but a cluster of them can quickly become dangerous
  • Wide-sweeping weapons like the Nightstar are effective when multiple Goopers group up
  • Take them out fast before they have a chance to surround you
Source: minathehollower.wiki.gg
⚡

Shock Trooper

Basic

Shock Troopers are the base enemy type that serves as the foundation for the more dangerous Hulk Trooper variant. These electrified soldiers patrol the island and attack with swift, charged strikes. While individually manageable, they become significantly more dangerous when enhanced into their Hulk form.

Type
Electric Soldier
Threat
Medium
Variant
Hulk Trooper

Combat Tips

  • Watch for their charged attack animations
  • Burrow to dodge their electric strikes
  • They are the base form of the more dangerous Hulk Trooper
Source: minathehollower.wiki.gg
🐟

Killer Fish

Basic

Killer Fish are large aquatic predators lurking in the murky waters of Nox's Bayou. These enemies patrol the swamp waters and can surprise players who venture too close to the surface. They are particularly dangerous near lilypads and wooden walkways where players must navigate carefully.

Type
Aquatic Predator
Location
Nox's Bayou
Threat
Medium-High

Combat Tips

  • Be cautious near water surfaces in Nox's Bayou
  • They may ambush from below when crossing lilypads
  • Burrowing can help avoid water-based attacks
  • Use ranged weapons like Battery Buster when possible
Source: 17173 Review - "Giant Killer Fish"
🎃

Scarecrow Stalker

Basic

Scarecrow Stalkers are eerie enemies found in the Halloween-themed Septemburg area. These scarecrow-like creatures relentlessly pursue players through the hedge mazes and autumn fields. Their behavior is tied to the area's dynamic lightning strikes, making them particularly unpredictable during storms.

Type
Halloween Creature
Location
Septemburg
Behavior
Tracking/Pursuing

Combat Tips

  • They continuously track and pursue you through the area
  • Watch for behavior changes during lightning strikes
  • Use hedge maze paths to create distance
  • Burrow to escape when cornered
Source: 17173 Review - "Scarecrow-style Stalker"
⚔️

Armored Knight

Basic

Armored Knights are heavily armored enemies found in the areas surrounding Ossex and Queensbury Crypt. These foes feel nearly invincible at starting levels, presenting a significant challenge early in the game. IGN's review specifically noted them as a major difficulty hurdle that can be overcome by leveling up attack stats.

Type
Armored Soldier
Threat
High (Early Game)
Weakness
Upgraded Attack

Combat Tips

  • These enemies feel nearly invincible at starting levels
  • Grind for Bones and upgrade your attack power before engaging
  • Use the burrow to dodge their attacks and reposition
  • They become much more manageable after a few attack upgrades
Source: IGN Review - "a particularly invincible-feeling set of knight enemies"

Molten Foundry Enemies

Area Group

The Molten Foundry area features a noticeably higher enemy density compared to preceding regions. Enemies are configured around a high-temperature smelting facility theme, with diverse enemy types and complex terrain combining platform jumping sections with heat wave traps. This area serves as a gauntlet that tests players' mastery of movement and combat under environmental pressure.

Location
Molten Foundry
Theme
High-Temp Smelting
Status
CONFIRMED

Area Hazards

  • Platform jumping sections combined with heat wave traps

Mimic

Basic

Mimics are deceptive enemies found in the secret underwater passage of the Eastern Heath area. These creatures disguise themselves as treasure chests or collectibles, luring unsuspecting players into close range before revealing their true form. Defeating a Mimic rewards the player with a Kear, making them a worthwhile encounter for those who can identify and overcome their ambush tactics.

Type
Mimic / Trap
Location
Eastern Heath (Underwater)
Drop
Kear

Combat Tips

  • Found in the secret underwater passage of Eastern Heath
  • Approach suspicious chests with caution -- they may be Mimics
  • Defeating them yields a valuable Kear
  • Use the burrow to dodge their surprise attack animation
Source: 100pguides.com

Scarecrow Merchants are special NPCs found in the Septemburg area who sell unique trinkets. Unlike hostile scarecrow enemies, these merchants offer valuable items for purchase, including the Repelling Root trinket. They can be found in special map areas within Septemburg's hedge mazes and autumn fields.

Brandish / Holden

NPC / Merchant

Brandish is a blue-glowing NPC found in Ossex's Bowery district, located above the Music Hall on the west side of town. Brandish has an alternate personality called Holden that emerges when Mina's Sidearms skill reaches Level 5. Holden sells the Dual Sidearm License for 2,000 Bones, which unlocks a second sidearm slot allowing Mina to carry two different sidearms simultaneously.

Type
Friendly NPC (Dual Personality)
Location
Ossex, Bowery District
Sells
Dual Sidearm License (2,000 Bones)
Requirement
Sidearms Skill Level 5

Interaction Guide

  • Find Brandish in the Bowery district above the Music Hall in west Ossex
  • He initially appears as a blue-glowing NPC with limited dialogue
  • After raising Sidearms to Level 5, talk to him again to trigger his transformation into Holden
  • Holden offers the Dual Sidearm License for 2,000 Bones
  • Don't rush -- focus on leveling Sidearms naturally and save Bones before returning
Source: 163.com (NetEase Games)

Brittle Beak

Basic

Brittle Beaks are scavenging bird enemies that appear after larger predators have finished feeding. These opportunistic creatures swoop down to clean up remaining bones and can catch players off guard with their diving attacks. A couple of precise hits are enough to take them down, but they must be dealt with quickly before they knock you into pits or hazards.

Type
Scavenging Bird
Threat
Low-Medium
Weakness
Few hits to defeat

Combat Tips

  • Take them out quickly with precise attacks — 1-2 hits suffice
  • Don't ignore them or they'll dive and knock you into pits
  • They appear after larger enemies feed, so stay alert after tough fights
Source: minathehollower.wiki.gg

Hulk Trooper

Mini-Boss

The Hulk Trooper is a beefed-up variant of the standard Shock Trooper, noticeably larger and far more aggressive. Thanks to its imposing size and surprising speed, it can close the distance quickly and strike across a wide area, making it tough to escape once it locks onto you.

Type
Enhanced Trooper
Base Form
Shock Trooper
Threat
High
Note: The Hulk Trooper is an enhanced version of the "Shock Trooper" base enemy type. The Shock Trooper serves as the foundation for this more dangerous variant.

Combat Tips

  • Its bulk and quick movement give it an unusually long attack reach
  • Trying to simply run away is not effective -- it will hunt you down relentlessly
  • Dodge its strikes by burrowing underground and repositioning
  • Wait for it to finish an attack animation, then punish the recovery window
  • The Blaststrike Maul's charged attack grants invincibility frames, letting you absorb a hit while dealing heavy damage
Source: minathehollower.wiki.gg
🐙

Nether Kraken

Boss

The Nether Kraken is a massive boss-level enemy encountered in the tutorial area of Loner's Landing. This colossal sea creature serves as the game's opening boss fight, testing players on the core combat and burrow mechanics early on. Despite being a tutorial encounter, the Nether Kraken presents a genuine challenge with its sweeping tentacle attacks and area-denial patterns. Defeating it rewards the Uranium Bracelet trinket, a valuable accessory for the rest of the adventure.

Type
Boss / Sea Creature
Location
Loner's Landing (Tutorial)
Reward
Uranium Bracelet

Combat Tips

  • First boss encounter in the game -- learn the burrow dodge timing here
  • Watch for sweeping tentacle attacks and reposition accordingly
  • Use the burrow to avoid area-denial patterns
  • Defeating it grants the Uranium Bracelet trinket
Source: 100pguides.com

Stone Statue

Basic

Stone Statues are enemies summoned by the Carving Man boss in the Kindlewood area. These statues attack alongside their master and must be dealt with during the Carving Man boss encounter. The Carving Man himself is invincible during the first encounter and must be pushed to the end of the area before the real fight unlocks.

Type
Summoned Enemy
Location
Kindlewood (Carving Man fight)
Threat
Medium

Combat Tips

  • Summoned continuously by the Carving Man during combat
  • Focus on dodging statue attacks while looking for openings on the Carving Man
  • First encounter is a chase sequence -- you cannot damage the Carving Man, push him to the area end
Source: 18183.com full walkthrough, Bilibili video guides

Skeleton Army

Mini-Boss

Skeleton Armies are summoned by the hidden boss Immortal Evra. These skeletons attack with flanking patterns and can quickly overwhelm players who don't manage the crowd effectively. Evra is a hidden boss found in a mini dungeon accessed through a 3 Plasma Vial door on the courtyard east side.

Type
Summoned Undead
Summoner
Immortal Evra (Hidden Boss)
Threat
High (in groups)

Combat Tips

  • Watch for flanking patterns -- skeletons approach from multiple angles
  • Use wide-area attacks like Nightstar or Blaststrike Maul to handle groups
  • Burrow to reposition when surrounded
Source: Kakuchopurei "ALL BOSS FIGHTS" video

Frog Band

NPC / Event

Frog Band is a group of musician frogs found in the Backwaters area. After completing the Frog Escort Event in Nox's Bayou, players can reunite a lost frog with its band. Paying 100 Bones sends the band to perform at Ossex, unlocking the Music Hall boss encounters. The frog escort is found behind a waterfall in Nox's Bayou after defeating the Nox Beast.

Type
NPC Event
Location
Nox's Bayou / Backwaters
Unlocks
Music Hall Bosses

Event Guide

  • After defeating Nox Beast, find the frog behind a waterfall in Nox's Bayou
  • Escort the frog through obstacles back to the shortcut area
  • Hit the frog northward in Backwaters to reunite it with the band
  • Pay 100 Bones to send the band to Ossex
Source: 163.com Hidden Boss Guide, gcores.com review
