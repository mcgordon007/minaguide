Eldritch Horror Boss
💀

The Duchess of Queensbury

Queensbury Crypt

The Duchess of Queensbury is an eldritch horror that serves as the boss of the Queensbury Crypt dungeon. As a being of cosmic terror, the Duchess embodies the dark, gothic themes of the crypt with a design that blends aristocratic elegance with grotesque, otherworldly horror.

The fight takes place within the deepest chambers of the crypt, where the Duchess commands the undead and unleashes eldritch attacks that fill the arena. This encounter tests the player's mastery of the burrow mechanic, as dodging her wide-area attacks requires precise underground movement and well-timed pop-up strikes.

The Duchess may be connected to the Duke, a timid character searching for his lost love. Whether the Duchess truly is the Duke's long lost companion or merely masquerading as such remains a dark mystery. (Source: minathehollower.wiki.gg)

Community Tips (Unverified)

These tips are based on general game mechanics and community discussion. They have not been verified against the actual boss fight.

  1. Watch for telegraph animations: Most bosses in the game signal their attacks ahead of time. Pay close attention to the Duchess's movements so you can react with a well-timed burrow to avoid damage.
  2. Stay underground during big attacks: The burrow grants invincibility frames, making it your best defensive option against wide-reaching attacks. Drop below the surface when the Duchess winds up, then resurface during the recovery window to land hits.
  3. Pop up for aerial strikes: Emerging from a burrow launches Mina upward, and the resulting aerial attack deals bonus damage. Position yourself beneath the Duchess before surfacing to capitalize on this extra damage window.
  4. Keep your Plasma bar active: Since attacking refills your Plasma (which converts to healing), staying on the offensive is its own form of defense. Look for safe moments between the Duchess's attack strings to land a few hits and sustain yourself.
  5. Equip helpful Trinkets: Depending on your approach, Trinkets that boost damage output or increase survivability can make a noticeable difference. Experiment with different loadouts to find what suits your playstyle.
  6. Carry Sparks for safety: If you have Sparks available, consider using them before learning a new boss. They prevent Bone loss on death, removing the penalty of trial-and-error while you study the encounter.

Hidden Boss 2: Maxi

Ossex Ambush (return from Queensbury Crypt)
CONFIRMED

Maxi is an ambush boss that triggers when you return to Ossex after completing the first dungeon in Queensbury Crypt. Enter the town from the right side to trigger the encounter.

Maxi's signature move is burrowing underground frequently. The recommended strategy is to use your own burrow ability to counter his -- when Maxi digs, burrow yourself to avoid damage and reposition for a counterattack. Matching his burrow timing is key to winning this fight.

Defeating Maxi rewards the Trinket Pouch, a highly useful item that expands your trinket equip slots, allowing you to equip multiple trinkets simultaneously. This makes Maxi a priority fight for players looking to maximize their build options.

Location
Ossex (right entrance)
Key Mechanic
Burrow Counter
Reward
Trinket Pouch
Trigger
Return from Queensbury

Strategy Tips

  1. Use burrow to counter burrow: When Maxi digs underground, immediately burrow yourself. This avoids his attack and lets you pop up for a counterstrike.
  2. Stay mobile: Don't stay in one spot for too long. Maxi's burrow attacks target your last position.
  3. Priority fight: The Trinket Pouch is extremely valuable. Consider doing this early to unlock multi-trinket builds.
Source: NetEase 163 game guide, Kakuchopurei gameplay footage

Hidden Boss 3: Armand

Ossex Blacksmith (Legovich's Shop)
CONFIRMED

Armand is a functional boss encountered at the Ossex blacksmith shop run by Legovich. The fight triggers when you attempt to purchase a second item from Legovich. Armand has extremely high damage reduction, making normal attacks nearly ineffective.

Exploit Strategy: Use your burrow ability to redirect lava onto Armand's position. Once he steps on the lava, his damage reduction is removed and he becomes vulnerable to normal attacks. This is the intended or at least most effective way to deal with his otherwise impenetrable defense.

Defeating Armand unlocks the blacksmith shop for normal operation, making this a functionally important boss fight rather than one that just grants a trinket.

New Info: The 163.com weapon guide confirms that Armand is the blacksmith who blocks purchases. The fight triggers on your second purchase attempt. His difficulty is described as "hidden boss level" — if you lose to him, it means your equipment and skills need more work. The blacksmith is named Legovich and his shop is in the red building in central Ossex.

Strategy Tips

  1. Burrow to redirect lava: Armand's high damage reduction makes direct combat impractical. Burrow near lava pools and guide the lava flow to his feet.
  2. Attack after lava contact: Once Armand stands on lava, his damage reduction drops. This is your window to deal real damage.
  3. Functional reward: Winning restores the blacksmith, giving you access to shop services. This is essential for progression.

Hidden Boss 4: Darkin

Ossex High Street (Random Spawn)
CONFIRMED

Darkin is a randomly spawning boss near Ossex High Street. Two children named Ak and Dak will mention Darkin, providing the clue to his existence. He can appear in one of two possible locations: near the south outskirts (close to the sunbathing man) or in the western hills (before entering Backwater).

Darkin is unique in that you must defeat him twice to truly beat him. After the first victory, he will reappear and must be fought again. Only after the second defeat is he considered fully vanquished.

The reward for defeating Darkin is the Lace Glove. Due to the random spawn mechanic, you may need to leave and re-enter the area multiple times to encounter him.

Strategy Tips

  1. Talk to Ak and Dak: These two children near Ossex High Street will clue you in about Darkin's existence.
  2. Check both spawn locations: Darkin randomly appears at either the south outskirts (near the sunbathing man) or the western hills (before Backwater). If he's not at one, try the other.
  3. Prepare for two fights: You need to beat Darkin twice. Make sure you have enough healing items and Sparks before engaging.
  4. Be patient with respawning: If Darkin doesn't appear, leave the area and come back. The random spawn may take several attempts.

Hidden Boss 5: Frog Escort Event

Nox's Bayou (Post-Nox Beast)
CONFIRMED

After defeating the Nox Beast in Nox's Bayou, a hidden escort event becomes available. From the shortcut, go downward, follow the rope down, and look for a room behind a waterfall on the left. Inside you'll find a frog in a barrel that needs to be escorted.

Escort Route: Guide the frog through obstacles and enemies back to the shortcut area. From there, head to the Backwater region (the large swamp area with moving platforms). Hit the frog northward to send it toward a band of musician frogs so it can reunite with the band.

Completing the frog escort rewards the Tutu Ballet trinket. This event also serves as the prerequisite for the Music Hall Shark Pirate series -- after reuniting the frog band, pay 100 Bones to send the band to Ossex, which unlocks the Music Hall boss encounters in the Bowery District.

Location
Nox's Bayou (post-boss)
Type
Escort Event
Reward
Tutu Ballet
Unlocks
Music Hall Bosses

Strategy Tips

  1. Find the frog: After beating Nox Beast, use the shortcut, go down the rope, and check behind the left waterfall.
  2. Protect the frog: Clear enemies along the escort path before moving the frog forward. The frog is vulnerable and can be lost if hit too many times.
  3. Backwater destination: Take the frog to the large swamp area with moving platforms in Backwater and hit it north to reunite it with the band.
  4. Pay 100 Bones: After the reunion, pay 100 Bones to send the frog band to Ossex. This triggers the Music Hall Shark Pirate boss encounters.

New Info (June 5): After reuniting the frog with its band in Backwaters, you receive the Tutu Ballet trinket. Then talk to the frog again and pay 100 Bones to send the band to perform at Ossex. This payment is required to unlock the Music Hall boss chain in the Bowery District. If the frog gets lost during escort, it will reappear at the nearest shore.

General Boss Strategies

Universal tips that apply to all boss encounters

Core Principles for Every Boss Fight

  • 1

    Learn Attack Patterns

    Every boss has a set of attack patterns with clear telegraphs. Spend your first attempts observing rather than attacking. Once you recognize the tells, you can dodge consistently and find your openings.

  • 2

    Grind Bones for Upgrades

    If a boss feels too difficult, spend time in other areas grinding Bones. Use them at the Underlabs to increase your attack power, health, and other stats. Even a few upgrade levels can make a significant difference.

  • 3

    Adjust Your Trinkets

    Each boss may require a different Trinket loadout. For damage races, equip Attack Multiplier and offensive Trinkets. For survivability, use Health Boost and Prototype Spark. Experiment to find what works.

  • 4

    Master the Burrow Mechanic

    The burrow is your primary defensive tool in boss fights. It provides invincibility frames and lets you reposition quickly. Practice burrow-to-pop-up combos for both defense and offense.

  • 5

    Keep Plasma Vials Full

    Aggressive play is rewarded in Mina the Hollower. Attack enemies to fill your Plasma bar, which converts to red health. Don't play passively -- the more you hit, the more you heal.

  • 6

    Use Sparks as Insurance

    Sparks act as extra lives that prevent Bone loss on death. Before a tough boss, consider spending resources to acquire Sparks. They remove the penalty of learning a fight through failure.

  • 7

    Try a Different Area

    Remember, the game is fully non-linear. If a boss is giving you trouble, leave and explore a different area. You may find new weapons, Trinkets, or upgrades that make the fight much easier when you return.

  • 8

    Don't Fear Difficulty Modifiers

    The game includes an extensive assist menu. If you just want to experience the story or are stuck after many attempts, use the available modifiers. There is no penalty for adjusting difficulty.
