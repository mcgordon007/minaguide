Cookie Policy

Understanding how we use cookies to improve your experience

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

Cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website, remember your preferences, and improve your browsing experience. They do not harm your device and do not contain viruses or malware.

Types of Cookies We Use

1. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation and access to secure areas of the website. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.

2. Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This helps us improve our website structure and content.

3. Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are relevant to you and your interests. They are also used to limit the number of times you see an advertisement and help measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

Google AdSense Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other websites on the Internet.

How Google Uses Cookies

  • DoubleClick Cookie: Google uses the DoubleClick cookie to enable it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Personalization: Cookies help Google show you personalized ads based on your interests and browsing history.
  • Measurement: Cookies are used to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.
  • Fraud Prevention: Cookies help detect and prevent click fraud and other invalid activity.

Third-Party Advertising Partners

Google AdSense may use third-party vendors and ad networks to serve ads on our website. These third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically find these settings in the "Options" or "Preferences" menu of your browser. Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data

Your Options

You have several options for managing cookies:

  • Accept all cookies: Allow all cookies for the best browsing experience
  • Block third-party cookies: Only allow cookies from the website you're visiting
  • Block all cookies: Prevent all cookies (may affect website functionality)
  • Delete cookies: Clear cookies that have already been stored

Opting Out of Personalized Ads

If you want to opt out of personalized advertising, you can:

  • Visit Google Ad Settings to control personalized ads from Google
  • Visit Network Advertising Initiative to opt out of personalized ads from participating companies
  • Visit Digital Advertising Alliance for additional opt-out options

Cookie Duration

Cookies can remain on your device for different periods of time:

  • Session Cookies: These are temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: These remain on your device for a set period of time or until you manually delete them. They help us remember your preferences for future visits.
