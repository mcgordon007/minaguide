Items & Equipment

Complete catalog of all weapons, sidearms, trinkets, and items in Mina the Hollower.

⚠️ This guide is actively updated with post-release information. New trinkets, hidden shops, and upgrade details added from player discoveries.

Key Points

  • ✓ 5 weapons: 3 starting + 2 unlockable, each with unique upgrade
  • ✓ Sidearms consume Joules and are lost on death - use wisely
  • ✓ Up to 5 Trinket slots for build customization
  • ✓ Bones are currency/XP - lost on death unless protected by Spark
  • ✓ Plasma heals through combat - attack to fill bar and recover HP
  • ✓ 3 hidden Poppit shops near Ossex sell rare collectibles (Soul Fires, Kael Artifacts)

Weapons

5 primary weapons, each with a unique moveset and upgrade path

Note: Three weapons are available from the start. The remaining two are unlockable through exploration. Each weapon can be upgraded at the Weapon Shop in Ossex.
💥

Nightstar

Starting Weapon Tier 1: Dread Mace Tier 2: Late Impact

The Nightstar is a chain mace/flail that serves as one of Mina's starting weapons. Its chain-based design provides extended reach compared to melee weapons, allowing Mina to strike enemies from a safer distance. The flail head swings in an arc, making it effective for hitting multiple enemies in a wide sweep.

The range advantage makes the Nightstar an excellent choice for players who prefer to keep their distance while maintaining aggressive offense. Its sweeping arc also helps when surrounded by enemies.

Whisper and Vesper

Starting Weapon Tier 1: Sky Dive Tier 2: Throwing Dagger

Whisper and Vesper are a pair of dual daggers available from the start. These fast-striking weapons prioritize speed over raw damage, allowing Mina to deliver rapid consecutive hits. The dual-wielding nature means attacks alternate between the two daggers, creating a flurry of strikes.

The high attack speed synergizes well with the Plasma health system, as more hits mean faster health regeneration. The daggers are ideal for aggressive players who want to maximize their time on the offensive.

Blaststrike Maul

Starting Weapon Tier 1: Blast Charge Tier 2: Spin Dash

The Blaststrike Maul is a heavy hammer available from the start. It trades speed for raw power, dealing significant damage per hit. Hold attack to charge up. While charging, move to roll. The roll is invulnerable for a period. This makes the Maul a defensive tool as well as an offensive one.

Time your charges to tank through enemy attacks while delivering devastating blows. The invincibility frames on the charged attack and roll make this weapon invaluable for boss fights.

Hollower's Shield

Unlockable Upgrade: Shield Toss

The Hollower's Shield is an unlockable weapon that provides defensive capabilities alongside offensive options. Tap to bash, hold to block. Bash as you get hit to parry. Watch the cracks on your shield so it doesn't break. The parry mechanic rewards precise timing with a devastating riposte.

The Shield's upgrade, Shield Toss, adds a ranged attack option, transforming the defensive tool into a versatile offensive weapon as well.

Battery Buster

Unlockable Tier 1: Burst Fire Tier 2: Charge Shot

The Battery Buster is an unlockable blaster weapon that provides ranged combat capabilities. A dynamic blaster. Jump + Attack to switch modes. Strike foes to power up, then fire! Ricochet shots to stay safe. Unlike the other melee-focused weapons, the Battery Buster allows Mina to engage enemies from a distance, adding a new dimension to combat strategy.

The Charge Shot upgrade enables a powerful ranged attack that deals increased damage and may have special properties such as piercing or area-of-effect damage.

Sidearms

Sub-weapons that consume Joules and are lost on death

Important: Sidearms consume Joules to use. They are lost upon death, so use them wisely. Sidearms are found by breaking objects throughout the world -- smash everything you see to discover them.

Gyro Dagger Confirmed

Boomerang Dagger

When thrown, the Gyro Dagger arcs outward and then curves back to Mina like a boomerang, striking enemies multiple times as it spins. A neat trick: you can hit the returning dagger to send it flying toward enemies again, effectively juggling it for extra damage.

Consumes Joules. Lost on death.
Source: minathehollower.wiki.gg

Trinkets

Equippable accessories -- up to 5 slots available

Key Items

Important unlockables and progression items

Dual Sidearm License

A key unlockable that grants Mina a second sidearm slot, allowing her to carry two different sidearms simultaneously. This significantly expands combat flexibility by letting you switch between sidearms on the fly.

  • NPC: Brandish / Holden
  • Location: Ossex, west side, Bowery district, above the Music Hall
  • Cost: 2,000 Bones
  • Requirement: Sidearms skill at Level 5
  • Controls: Press E (keyboard) or RB/R2 (controller) to switch between sidearms

Tip: Don't rush to Brandish early. Focus on leveling your Sidearms skill naturally through combat and save up 2,000 Bones before returning to purchase the license.

Confirmed
Source: 163.com (NetEase Games)

Other Items

Consumables, currency, and key items

Keygears

Universal keys used to unlock doors and gates throughout Tenebrous Isle. Keygears open paths to new areas and secret rooms. Explore thoroughly and break objects to find them.

Joule Jars

Consumable items that replenish Joules, the resource consumed by Sidearms. Carry Joule Jars when you plan to use Sidearms frequently, especially during boss fights.

Bones

The primary currency and experience points of the game. Bones are earned by defeating enemies and are used to purchase items from shops and upgrade Mina's abilities at Underlabs. Bones are lost on death unless protected by a Spark.

Sparks

Extra lives that prevent Bone loss upon death. When Mina dies with a Spark active, she revives without losing her accumulated Bones. Sparks are invaluable for learning difficult boss encounters risk-free.

Plasma Vials

The healing system of Mina the Hollower. Mina has a yellow Plasma bar that fills by attacking enemies. Once full, it converts into red health. This system rewards aggressive play -- the more you attack, the more you heal. Similar to the glory kill system in DOOM 2016.

Map

Allows Mina to view an area overview map of Tenebrous Isle from the Underlab. Purchase the base map from Drillhardt at the Hollowers' Guild for 500 Bones. An enhanced version can be purchased for another 500 Bones, which displays collectible completion progress for each area.

Confirmed
Source: 163.com Player Guide

Hidden Shops

Secret Poppit shops near Ossex

Poppit Shops: Three hidden Poppit shops are located around Ossex, marked by blue torches. Each shop sells one Kear (key) and one Wisp trinket — considered among the best trinkets in the game and priced affordably. Finding all three shops and buying out the inventory of the last one you discover triggers a hidden event revealing Poppit's backstory. Poppit remains at each location indefinitely until his stock is depleted, so you can return later if short on Bones.

Poppit Shop #1 — Eastern Wilds

From Ossex, head east into the Grassland area, then turn south (do NOT cross the bridge). Navigate past a spiked gear ring, take the first right, cross a moving platform room, then continue east. At the end you'll find an area with three spiked gears and a purple doorway in the bottom-right. Burrow through to find Poppit waiting on the right side of the cave.

Poppit Shop #2 — Molten Foundry

From Ossex, head west to the red-ground lava block area. Find the second north exit. If blocked by red boulders, stand on a tile adjacent to lava and burrow toward the boulder — the lava flow will destroy it. Head northeast into the Molten Foundry. Inside, find the gap in the northeast corner, burrow through, follow the metal grating, pass under the door, and take the left path at the fork.

Poppit Shop #3 — Cave Network

Reach through cave network to right-side stairs, head south, clear enemies. Find a jet plant near a house, burrow under it and launch west to reach a southern ledge. Meet Poppit, learn a dance (required for 100% completion), and unlock teleport blocks for fast travel. The third shop's exact location depends on the order you discover the first two shops.
