+

Game Mechanics

What is the burrow mechanic in Mina the Hollower?

+

The burrow is Mina's signature ability that lets her travel underground. It serves multiple functions: dodging attacks with invincibility frames, traversing beneath obstacles, discovering hidden secrets, and launching pop-up aerial attacks that deal bonus damage. The burrow replaces traditional dodge rolls found in other action games.

How does the Plasma healing system work?

+

Mina has a yellow Plasma bar that fills when attacking enemies. Once full, it converts into red health (actual HP recovery). This system rewards aggressive play - the more you attack, the more you heal. It is inspired by DOOM 2016's glory kill mechanic but works continuously through combat rather than requiring specific finishing moves.

What are Bones in Mina the Hollower?

+

Bones are the primary currency and experience points in Mina the Hollower. They are earned by defeating enemies and can be spent at Underlabs for permanent stat upgrades or used to purchase items from shops. Bones are lost upon death unless protected by a Spark, creating a risk-reward progression system similar to Souls games.

How do Underlabs work?

+

Underlabs are save points and upgrade hubs scattered throughout Tenebrous Isle. Resting at an Underlab saves progress and lets you spend Bones on permanent stat upgrades. However, resting also respawns all enemies in the world. Underlabs are essential for progression but require strategic timing.

What are Trinkets in Mina the Hollower?

+

Trinkets are equippable accessories that provide passive bonuses. You can equip up to 5 Trinkets simultaneously for build customization. Examples include Ferrying Wisp (floating), Prototype Spark (revive on death), Attack Multiplier (bonus damage), and Health Boost (increased HP). Different Trinket combinations suit different playstyles and situations.

World & Exploration

How many areas are in Mina the Hollower?

+

Tenebrous Isle contains 7 distinct areas: Ossex (central hub town), Queensbury Crypt, Nox's Bayou, Septemburg, Bone Beach, Frozen Trainyard, and Astral Orrery. Each area has its own unique theme, enemies, and challenges.

What is Tenebrous Isle?

+

Tenebrous Isle is the gothic, interconnected game world where Mina the Hollower takes place. It is a dark island divided into 7 distinct areas with unique themes. The island's lore involves stolen spark generators that Mina must recover, with various antagonists controlling different regions.

What are Spark Generators?

+

Spark Generators are massive machines designed by Mina to bring electricity and prosperity to Tenebrous Isle. There are 6 Spark Generators located throughout the dungeon areas, and repairing them is the main objective of the game. As Mina investigates, she discovers darker secrets about the generators' true purpose and the forces that seek to control them.

Who is Thorne?

+

Thorne is a rebellious bat soldier who serves as the leader of the antagonistic forces on Tenebrous Isle. As the main antagonist, Thorne opposes Mina's efforts to repair the Spark Generators. The character has complex motivations that go beyond simple villainy, with promotional material suggesting a nuanced role in the story.

Does Mina the Hollower have New Game+?

+

Yes, Mina the Hollower features New Game+ mode after completing the main story. Players can continue exploring with their progress carried over, and subsequent playthroughs offer unique difficulty scaling combinations. The game supports up to NG+7, providing significant replay value for players who want to experience the adventure with increased challenge.

What accessibility options are available?

+

Mina the Hollower offers extensive accessibility features unlocked from the start, making it one of the most inclusive games in its genre. Players can adjust speed, increase jump height, reduce damage taken, and modify various difficulty parameters. This allows players of all skill levels to experience the game's content without barriers.

Is Mina the Hollower a Metroidvania?

+

Mina the Hollower has Metroidvania elements but is not a traditional Metroidvania. It features an interconnected world and non-linear exploration, but unlike Metroidvanias, there are no ability gates or required items to access areas. All 7 areas are accessible from the start, making it more of an open-world action RPG.

Can you play Mina the Hollower in any order?

+

Yes, Mina the Hollower is fully non-linear. All 6 dungeon areas beyond Ossex can be tackled in any order with no ability locks or required sequence. If an area feels too difficult, you can leave and explore elsewhere, returning later with more upgrades and experience.

Does Mina the Hollower have a detailed map?

+

No, Mina the Hollower intentionally does not provide a detailed room-by-room map. This design choice makes secret discovery more challenging and rewarding. However, Underlabs can be outfitted with a rudimentary map that tracks collectibles by region. The IGN reviewer noted this significantly ups the challenge of finding secrets in a fun way.

What is the point of no return in Mina the Hollower?

+

The game has a clearly labeled point of no return before the final area. This allows you to complete 100% exploration and collect all items before proceeding to the ending. Additionally, your completion rate and discovered treasure carry over into New Game+, so you can continue hunting for secrets on subsequent playthroughs.

How does the Bone savings account work?

+

When spending Bones at an Underlab, you have four options: upgrade main weapon attack, upgrade defense, upgrade Sidearm attack, or store Bones in a "savings account." The savings account is impervious to death, meaning your stored Bones are completely safe even if you die. This provides a way to protect your progress without needing to spend Sparks.

How do I get the map in Mina the Hollower?

+

The map is acquired by completing an escort quest. Enter the Hollower's Guild (right of Ossex's main gate, break boxes on the right side), talk to Muriel inside, then head south from Ossex with a Kear (key). Navigate through the burned town, open a yellow door marked "1" with a Plasma Vial, and rescue Rhene. Escort him back safely — he cannot burrow and can only jump 1 tile wide gaps. Once rescued, the map becomes available at the Guild and can later be upgraded to show a collectible checklist per region.

What are the Poppit hidden shops?

+

Three hidden Poppit shops are located around Ossex, marked by blue torches. They sell rare collectibles including Soul Fires and Kael Artifacts at low prices. Finding all three shops lets you meet Poppit, learn a dance (required for 100% completion), and unlock a manual fast-travel system using teleportation blocks. The shops are found by exploring gaps in railings, foundry areas, and cave networks around Ossex.

What is the best way to farm Bones?

+

The most efficient early-game Bone farming route is at Knight's Rest in Mourner's Mile (east of Ossex). Use the Underlab reset mechanic: enter and leave an Underlab to respawn all enemies and breakable objects. A simple route involves smashing 3 Bone-filled boulders near the northern Underlab entrance repeatedly. An advanced route adds Armored Knight kills and extends to a second Underlab near Queensbury Crypt. The Deboning Wand trinket boosts Bone drops by ~50% but scatters them.

How do I get the map? (Detailed)

+

First, find and rescue Rhene from the southern outskirts building (she's captured by Thorne's forces). Heal her with a Plasma Vial, then escort her to the Hollowers' Guild. Once there, attack the vines 3 times to summon Drillhardt, who sells the map for 500 Bones. An enhanced version (500 Bones) tracks collectibles per area. The map can only be viewed in your Underlab.

Source: 163.com (NetEase) Player Guide

What is the recommended dungeon order?

+

While the game is fully non-linear, Yacht Club Games recommends: Northeast (Queensbury Crypt) first for the Proto Spark trinket, then Southwest, Northwest, and finally Southeast. This order provides the smoothest difficulty curve.

Source: Yacht Club Games official recommendation

What happens if I don't save the children in Septemburg?

+

In the Septemburg area, you'll find children trapped in dungeons. Choosing not to rescue them has negative consequences later in the game. GameSpot editors who skipped this regretted it. It's highly recommended to take the time to save them.

Source: GameSpot Editorial, 163.com (NetEase) Player Guide

How does the mirror teleportation system work?

+

Mirrors throughout Tenebrous Isle are not just decorative — they form a connected teleportation network. Discovering and using mirrors can unlock shortcuts and hidden paths across the island.

Source: 163.com (NetEase) Player Guide

Weapons & Combat

What weapons are available in Mina the Hollower?

+

Mina the Hollower features 5 primary weapons: Nightstar (chain mace), Whisper and Vesper (dual daggers), Blaststrike Maul (heavy hammer), Hollower's Shield (parry shield), and Battery Buster (ranged blaster). Three weapons are available from the start, while two are unlockable through exploration. Each weapon has unique movesets and upgrade paths.

What is the best weapon in Mina the Hollower?

+

There is no single best weapon - each excels in different situations. Nightstar offers range and crowd control. Whisper and Vesper provide fast attacks for Plasma generation. Blaststrike Maul grants invincibility frames on charged attacks. Hollower's Shield enables parries. Battery Buster offers ranged damage. The best weapon depends on your playstyle and the situation.

How do weapon upgrades work?

+

Each weapon has two possible upgrades. After acquiring a weapon, picking up another of the same type upgrades it. For example, the Nightstar upgrades to the Dread Mace, and the Blaststrike Maul upgrades to the Explosive Charge. Upgrades grant new abilities and enhanced movesets.

Does the Hollower's Shield have durability?

+

Yes, the Hollower's Shield has a durability system shown by visible cracks. If the shield takes too much damage, it can break during battle. Watch the cracks on your shield to manage its durability. The shield can bash (tap attack), block (hold attack), and parry (bash as you get hit).

What is the best upgrade to prioritize?

+

For new players, prioritize Defense to at least level 4 first. The burrow mechanic takes time to master, and extra defense provides crucial survivability during the learning phase. After stabilizing defense, switch to Attack or Sidearm upgrades based on your preferred playstyle. Avoid converting all Bones to Bonestone early — the game's difficulty curve is front-loaded, and early upgrades matter more than safe storage.

What is the Sidearm Recoverer?

+

The Sidearm Recoverer is an upgrade purchasable from Drillhardt at the Hollower's Guild. It prevents Sidearms from being lost upon death, which is a significant quality-of-life improvement. With this upgrade, Sidearms shift from disposable emergency tools to a sustainable combat resource worth investing Bones into.

Bosses

How many bosses are in Mina the Hollower?

+

Mina the Hollower features 20+ boss encounters. The 6 dungeon bosses are: The Duchess of Queensbury, Nox Beast (Nox's Bayou), Maxi (Septemburg), Madd House + Miner Dwarf Boss (Bone Beach), Brain Boss (Frozen Trainyard), and Congulant (Astral Orrery). Thorne, the main antagonist, is fought 3 times throughout the story. Additional encounters include Tower Climb sequences, the Tentacle Boss, Rabbit Boss, Snow Mountain Bosses, Radiant Manor Bosses, and the Final Boss.

Who is the first boss in Mina the Hollower?

+

Since the game is fully non-linear, there is no designated first boss. You can challenge any dungeon boss in any order. The Duchess of Queensbury in Queensbury Crypt is one confirmed boss that players may encounter early if they choose that area first.

Items & Equipment

Where is the Proto Spark trinket?

+

The Proto Spark is found in the Northeast dungeon (Queensbury Crypt). Help The Duke return to his coffin to receive it. It grants one free revival on death — your Sparks and Bones are preserved. It's considered one of the most useful trinkets in the game.

Source: Kakuchopurei Video, 163.com (NetEase) Player Guide

Where is the Primed Vial Pouch?

+

Found in a house south of Ossex. Don't enter through the front door — climb in through the chimney instead. It lets you carry 2 additional Plasma Vials, significantly improving early-game survivability.

Source: 163.com (NetEase) Player Guide

How do I expand my trinket slots?

+

Defeat the hidden boss Maxi by entering Ossex from the right side after completing the Queensbury Crypt. The reward is a Trinket Pouch that expands your accessory slots.

Source: 163.com (NetEase) Player Guide, Kakuchopurei Video

How many hidden bosses are there?

+

There are 9 confirmed hidden bosses outside the main story. They include the Ancestor Chamber boss, Maxi, Armand (blacksmith), Darkin (random spawn), the Frog Escort event, and the Music Hall Shark Pirate series. Some have random spawn conditions or multi-step requirements.

Source: Kakuchopurei Video, 163.com (NetEase) Player Guide
